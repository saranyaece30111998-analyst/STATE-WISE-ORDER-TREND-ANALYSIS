#  📊 Statewise Business Orders Trends Analysis – India Market

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **order trends across Indian states, product categories, and sub-categories** using E‑commerce data (2018–2019).  
The unified dashboard consolidates KPIs to provide a holistic view of **sales, profitability, and target achievement**, enabling stakeholders to evaluate performance and identify actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Evaluate product category performance (Clothing, Furniture, Electronics) against sales targets.  
- Analyze correlation between **sales volume vs profitability** to detect loss-making patterns.  
- Compare **order distribution across states** to highlight high-demand and underperforming regions.  
- Track **monthly sales trends** to identify seasonal fluctuations.  
- Assess contribution of sub-categories (Printers, Sarees, Phones, etc.) to overall revenue.  
- Visualize **geographic sales distribution** to support regional marketing strategies.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights (SMART + Analytics Approach)

### Descriptive
- Clothing & Furniture nearly met targets; Electronics fell short by 4K.  
- Maharashtra (87 orders) & Madhya Pradesh (82) lead; Uttar Pradesh lags.  
- Printers (58K), Bookcases (57K), Sarees (54K) drive revenue.  
- Bangalore, Ahmedabad, Delhi dominate sales; Tier‑2 cities underutilized.  

### Diagnostic
- Electronics underperformed → weak demand/pricing issues.  
- High volumes ≠ consistent profits → discount inefficiencies.  
- Seasonal decline: sales dropped from 61K to 13K.  

### Predictive
- Clothing & Furniture expected to sustain; Electronics may continue weak.  
- Tier‑2 cities show **20% growth potential** in next year.  
- Printers, Bookcases, Sarees remain top contributors.  

### Prescriptive
- Strengthen **promotions for Electronics** to close target gap.  
- Optimize **discounting & cost structures** for profitability.  
- Launch **targeted campaigns** in weaker states (UP, Rajasthan).  
- Expand into **Tier‑2 cities** for untapped market potential.  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
The analysis shows strong performance in **Clothing & Furniture**, while **Electronics needs corrective strategies**.  
Regional demand is concentrated in major states and cities, with clear opportunities in **Tier‑2 markets**.  
Profitability and sales trends require optimization, guiding a roadmap for **balanced growth and sustainable expansion**.
